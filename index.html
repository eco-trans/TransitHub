Methodology

On-time performance
Observations with delay between −60 and +300 seconds, divided by all valid observations.
Mean delay
Observation-weighted mean. Positive values indicate late service.
Date coverage
Selected days with at least one observation, divided by selected days.
Source
Results are derived from publicly available real-time transit feeds for research and informational purposes. Estimates reflect the available data and may be affected by reporting errors or incomplete coverage. For questions or to report a potential error, contact luyuliu@auburn.edu.
